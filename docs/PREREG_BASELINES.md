# Preregistration: real wiring against matched null networks

- **Registered:** 2026-10-18. The commit that adds this file is the registration record. No null network had been simulated before it.
- **Status:** frozen. Do not edit this file after its runs. Changes go in dated amendments at the end.
- **Code:** `ascend/baselines.py` (networks, scoring), `scripts/baseline_check.py` (runs), `tests/test_baselines.py`.

## Question

The Shiu et al. 2024 model has no trained parameters. Its weights are the FlyWire synapse counts, signed by neurotransmitter, times one constant. Do its selective sensorimotor responses come from **which neuron connects to which**, or would any network with the same size and statistics produce them?

## Claim under test

> In the FlyWire v783 connectome-constrained model, the selective readout responses (sugar → MN9; Johnston's organ neurons → DNg62/DNge078; no cross-talk; bitter drives neither) depend on the specific wiring. Matched null networks with the same neurons, the same number of connections and the same signed weights do not produce them.

## Networks

All networks use the same 138,639 neurons, 15,091,983 connections and multiset of signed weights. Stimulus and readout neurons keep their identities.

| Variant | Kept | Destroyed | Shuffle seeds |
|---|---|---|---|
| `real` | everything | nothing | — |
| `weights` | every connection (who → whom) | which connection has which signed weight (so signs no longer follow the presynaptic neuron) | 0, 1, 2 |
| `rewired` | each neuron's in-degree, out-degree and outgoing signed weights (Dale's law) | which neuron each connection targets | 0, 1, 2 |
| `random` | only the totals and the weight multiset | everything else | 0, 1, 2 |

That makes 10 networks in total. `rewired` permutes the postsynaptic column across all connections, which can create self-connections and duplicate pairs. This is allowed and disclosed (about 0.002% self-connections for seed 0).

## Protocol (fixed)

- **Engine:** the ASCEND CPU engine in float32. It passed the Phase 1 F tier (F-f32), and float32 is the GPU's precision.
- **Conditions:** Shiu's stimulus sets at 150 Hz, run separately: `sugar` (21 GRNs), `bitter` (21 GRNs) and `jon_all` (146 JONs).
- **Trials:** 10 per condition and network, kick seeds 0–9, 1.0 s each. Every network gets the same kicks.
- **Readout groups** (`data/stimuli_783.json`):
  - **MN9** is the mean rate of `mn9_left` and `mn9_right`.
  - **JON_DN** is the mean rate of `jon_dn1` (DNg62) and `jon_dn2` (DNge078).
- **Runaway rule:** a trial is stopped once it passes 1,000,000 spikes, about 35 times the most active real condition. The condition is then marked runaway and its remaining trials are skipped.

## Score (fixed)

Over the 10 trials, a group is **on** if its mean rate is ≥ 10 Hz and **off** if it is below 1 Hz. Rates in between count as wrong whichever state was expected, and so does a runaway condition. The score is the number of the 6 cells below that match (0–6):

| Condition | MN9 | JON_DN |
|---|---|---|
| sugar | on | off |
| bitter | off | off |
| jon_all | off | on |

**Descriptive only (not scored):** spikes per trial, active neurons, readout rates, and the Pearson r of per-neuron rates against `results/phase0/reference_rates.json`.

## Claim rule

- **C1:** `real` scores 6/6. This is a matched positive control. Its outcome is close to known already, because Phase 1 F-f32 measured these rates over 30 trials. It is rerun here with the same protocol as the nulls.
- **C2:** every one of the 9 null networks scores below 6/6.

The claim holds only if C1 and C2 both hold. If any null network scores 6/6, the claim fails and the miss is reported.

## Predictions (stated before running)

- `rewired` and `random`: below 6/6 in every seed. Predicted failure modes are silence, or non-selective and possibly runaway activity.
- `weights`: below 6/6 in every seed, though with less confidence. Keeping the topology may preserve some routing, and the result says how much the signed strengths matter beyond who connects to whom.

## Descriptive benchmarks on the same machine (no pass/fail)

These are recorded with `scripts/bench.py` (5 × 1 s, sugar at 150 Hz, seeds 0–4) for the benchmark table in `docs/TECHNICAL_REPORT.md`:
- the ASCEND CPU engine in float64 and in float32;
- wall time per step;
- peak host memory.

The Brian2 reference figure is the Phase 0 measurement.

## Compute and hardware

The runs use the 4-vCPU build VM, which has no GPU. The estimate is about 10 networks × 3 conditions × 10 trials × ~7.5 s, roughly 40 minutes, run as up to 3 parallel processes. Every result file records the hardware it ran on.

## Output

- `results/baselines/null_networks.json`: summary JSON, under 1 MB.
- A section in `docs/TECHNICAL_REPORT.md`, with every network's score, misses included.
