# Preregistration: Phase 0 (reference baseline)

- **Registered:** 2026-10-18, before any of the measurements below were run. The commit that adds this file is the registration record.
- **Status:** frozen. Do not edit this file after its runs. Changes go in dated amendments at the end.

## Purpose

Before building a fast engine (Phase 1), fix what it has to match and how much faster it has to be:

1. Confirm that our harness runs the Shiu et al. 2024 reference model faithfully, and that the model reproduces its published sugar GRN → MN9 result on the FlyWire v783 connectome.
2. Generate reference firing rates that Phase 1 fidelity checks will compare against.
3. Measure the reference model's real-time factor (RTF) on the build VM, which sets the gap to 1.0.

## Model and data (fixed)

- **Model:** `ascend/reference/shiu_model.py`, a verbatim copy of `model.py` from philshiu/Drosophila_brain_model @ `91bdd1e` (MIT). Its SHA-256 is pinned in `tests/test_vendored.py`. It is a leaky integrate-and-fire network with alpha-shaped synaptic conductance. Parameters are the upstream `default_params`: w_syn 0.275 mV per synapse, 1.8 ms synaptic delay, 2.2 ms refractory period, τ_mbr 20 ms, τ 5 ms, Poisson stimulus weight 250 × w_syn, and Brian2's default dt of 0.1 ms.
- **Simulator:** Brian2 runtime mode with the Cython code generation target, on a single process (upstream's configuration).
- **Connectome:** FlyWire v783 as packaged by Shiu et al. (`Completeness_783.csv`, `Connectivity_783.parquet`): 138,639 neurons, 15,091,983 connections, 54,492,922 synapses. FlyWire v630 (`*_630_*`) is used only for the harness check (R1). SHA-256 for every file: `data/manifest.json`.
- **Stimulus neurons:** Shiu et al.'s sets (`data/stimuli_630.json`, copied from their `figures.ipynb`), mapped to v783 by `scripts/map_stimuli.py` and recorded in `data/stimuli_783.json`. Four of the IDs changed between releases. Each was matched by synaptic-partner similarity and accepted with a score of 0.69–0.94 against runner-up scores of 0.06–0.16, with cell types consistent with the set. Among them is the second MN9 (Shiu's "right" MN9, v630 `720575940645521262` → v783 `720575940618238523`, both FlyWire type CB0701).
- **Readouts:** `mn9_left` = `720575940660219265` and `mn9_right` = `720575940618238523` (v783). These use Shiu's names. FlyWire v783's `side` annotation labels them the other way round because of the v630 mirror convention.

## Protocol

- **Trials:** 30 trials of 1.0 s simulated time per condition, seeds 0–29, with the same seeds in every condition. Brian2 is seeded with `brian2.seed(seed)` immediately before each trial.
- **Build once, restore per trial:** each condition's network is built once and its initial state stored. Before each trial the stored state is restored. With the same seed, this gives spike trains identical to a fresh build. This was checked on the full v783 network in a pilot and is tested in `tests/test_reference.py`. One untimed warm-up step (then restore) precedes the trials.
- **Rates:** spike count / 1.0 s per trial. The mean and population standard deviation (ddof = 0) across the 30 trials follow upstream `utils.get_rate`.
- **Conditions** (frozen in `scripts/reference_rates.py`):

| Name | Data | Stimulus | Poisson rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| `v630_sugar_100hz` | v630 | 21 sugar GRNs | 100 Hz |
| `v630_sugar_200hz` | v630 | 21 sugar GRNs | 200 Hz |
| `v783_sugar_100hz` | v783 | 21 sugar GRNs | 100 Hz |
| `v783_sugar_150hz` | v783 | 21 sugar GRNs | 150 Hz (upstream default) |
| `v783_sugar_200hz` | v783 | 21 sugar GRNs | 200 Hz |
| `v783_bitter_150hz` | v783 | 21 bitter GRNs | 150 Hz |
| `v783_jon_all_150hz` | v783 | 146 Johnston's organ neurons (JO-CE, JO-F, JO-D/m) | 150 Hz |
| `v783_none` | v783 | none | none |

The three Phase 0 reference stimuli are therefore sugar GRNs, bitter GRNs and all Johnston's organ neurons (JONs), all at the upstream default of 150 Hz, plus no stimulus.

## Hypotheses and pass criteria: sugar GRN → MN9

Comparison values are Shiu et al.'s published model output: `results/example/sugarR.parquet` (200 Hz) and `sugarR_100Hz.parquet` (100 Hz) in their repository, FlyWire v630, 30 trials each. MN9 mean rates:

| Sugar rate | mn9_left | mn9_right |
|---|---|---|
| 200 Hz | 93.27 Hz | 61.80 Hz |
| 100 Hz | 67.03 Hz | 48.63 Hz |

- **R1, harness check (v630):** at 100 Hz and at 200 Hz, both MN9 means are within ±5 Hz of the published values (4 checks). The SE of a difference of two 30-trial means here is about 1–1.7 Hz, so a correct harness passes easily and a wrong one does not. If R1 fails, the harness is wrong: stop and report before interpreting anything else.
- **R2, qualitative (v783):**
  - sugar at 150 Hz drives both MN9s to ≥ 10 Hz;
  - no stimulus gives zero spikes in every trial (the model has no noise source);
  - bitter GRNs alone at 150 Hz keep both MN9s below 1 Hz.
- **R3, quantitative (v783):** at 100 Hz and at 200 Hz, both MN9 means are within ±25 % of the published v630 values (4 checks). The tolerance allows for edits to the connectome between releases.
- **R4, dose response (v783):** the mn9_left mean rises strictly from 100 to 150 to 200 Hz.

**Verdict:** "sugar → MN9 reproduces on v783" only if R1–R4 all pass. Each rule and check is reported separately, including failures. `scripts/reproduce_sugar_mn9.py` evaluates these criteria exactly as written here.

## Real-time factor (measurement, no pass criterion)

- **Definition:** RTF = simulated seconds / wall-clock seconds of `net.run()` for one trial. RTF ≥ 1.0 means real time. Network construction and the one-time warm-up are reported separately and are not part of the RTF.
- **Workload:** `scripts/bench.py` defaults: v783, 21 sugar GRNs at 150 Hz, 1.0 s per trial, 5 trials with seeds 0–4. Reported value: the median RTF, with its minimum and maximum. Gap to real time = 1 / median RTF.
- **Conditions:** the build VM, with no other heavy process running. Hardware and software are recorded automatically in the JSON.
- **Prediction:** RTF well below 1.0, of order 0.1, based on the pilot below.

## Pilots disclosed

These runs were made while building the harness, before this registration. They were used only for sizing, and none of them is a result:

- 0.1 s sugar trials (150 Hz, v783, seeds 0–1) while another heavy job was running: about 80 s per `net.run()`, mostly one-time setup. mn9_left fired at 60–70 Hz over those 100 ms.
- A Brian2 profile of a 0.05 s sugar trial showed that per-step cost is small next to the first-run setup.
- The fresh-build vs. store/restore identity check on 0.05 s trials.
- We also read Shiu et al.'s published v630 MN9 rates (table above) before writing the criteria.

The direction of sugar → MN9 on v783 was therefore already glimpsed in the pilot. The quantitative criteria (R1, R3, R4) and the bitter and no-stimulus predictions in R2 were not tested.

## Outputs

- `results/phase0/reference_rates.json`: every condition, with rates of all neurons that have a nonzero mean, readout spike counts per trial, timings and hardware.
- `results/phase0/sugar_mn9.json`: R1–R4 checks and the verdict.
- `results/phase0/bench_vm.json`: the RTF measurement.

Each output stays under 1 MB. Misses are published like hits.
