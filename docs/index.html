ASCEND

The whole adult fruit fly brain as a connectome-constrained model: 138,639 neurons wired by 15 million measured connections. It is built to run in real time on one consumer GPU.

What has been measured

Spike for spike

In double precision on the CPU, the ASCEND engine and the published Brian2 model of Shiu et al. 2024 emit identical spikes on the full brain for every stimulus tested.

0 trained

No parameter is fitted. The weights are synapse counts measured by electron microscopy, signed by neurotransmitter, times one constant.

6/6 vs 4/6

Real wiring routes sugar to the feeding motor neuron and antennal input to its descending neurons. Nine matched null networks with the same neurons and weights but shuffled wiring never do. This test was preregistered.

ASCEND builds on Shiu et al. 2024 (Nature 634) for the model; FlyWire (Dorkenwald et al. 2024; Schlegel et al. 2024) for the connectome; and NeuroMechFly/FlyGym, Eon Systems and erojasoficial-byte/fly-brain for related work.

This is a connectome-constrained model. It is not a brain upload, and it makes no claim to be conscious. MIT licensed.
